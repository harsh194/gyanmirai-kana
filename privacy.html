GyanMirai Kana App — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy covers the GyanMirai Kana mobile app for Android, listed on Google Play as Hiragana & Katakana: GyanMirai (the "App"), published by GyanMirai ("we," "our," or "us"). It explains what data the App uses, who else receives it, and the choices you have. For the gyanmirai.com website, see our website Privacy Policy.

1. The short version

  • The App works fully without an account. Signing in is optional, and versions of the App released before GyanMirai accounts were added have no sign-in at all.
  • Where the App offers sign-in, you sign in with your GyanMirai account: the same account works on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps that use GyanMirai accounts.
  • If you don't sign in, your lessons, reviews, favourites and settings are stored only on your device and we do not receive them.
  • If you sign in, we store your account (email address and basic profile details) and a copy of your learning progress, favourites and study settings on our servers, so they can be backed up and synced across your devices.
  • You can delete your account and its synced data in the App (You → Delete account) or at any time by email. Deleting your GyanMirai account deletes it everywhere: on gyanmirai.com and in every GyanMirai app.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID.
  • The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. Data stored on your device

Your study progress (which lessons you finished, which kana you have learned, review schedules and how your answers went), your favourites, the days you opened the App (for the visit streak) and your study settings are kept in a local database on your device. If you don't sign in, this data is never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it.

The App does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. Your handwriting in the writing exercises is checked on your device and is not saved or sent anywhere.

3. Your account (optional)

The App can let you sign in to back up your progress and sync it between devices. No feature of the App requires an account, and versions released before GyanMirai accounts were added have no sign-in.

One GyanMirai account

Signing in uses your GyanMirai account: the same account works on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps that use GyanMirai accounts. If you don't have one yet, signing in creates it. You sign in with Google or with a one-time code sent to your email address, whichever the App offers.

What we receive and store

  • Account details: if you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the App; with an email code, we receive only your email address. Our backend stores your account as a user record containing your email address, the sign-in method, a random user ID we assign and, if you use Google, a Google account identifier and the basic profile details Google provides.
  • gyanmirai.com profile: because the account is your GyanMirai account, it also has a gyanmirai.com profile, created automatically the first time you sign in, with your email address, your name and profile picture link (if Google provides them) and the sign-in method. What you do on gyanmirai.com is covered by our website Privacy Policy.
  • Learning progress: the review schedule for each kana you have studied, the lessons you have finished and when, which kana pictures you have already seen, your favourites and your study settings. This is linked to your user ID.
  • Not uploaded: the days you opened the App, your notification settings, the kana of the day and other preferences for this device stay on your phone.

How we use it

Only to provide the account: to sign you in, back up your progress and keep it in sync across your devices, to let you use the same account on gyanmirai.com and in our other apps, and to let your "Remove ads" purchase follow your account. We do not use account or progress data for advertising, and we do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

Where it is stored

Account and progress data are stored with Supabase, our backend provider, in its Tokyo (Japan) region, and are only accessible to your account. If you live outside Japan, this means your data is transferred to and processed in Japan. Supabase processes it on our behalf under its privacy policy.

Signing out and deleting your account

Signing out stops syncing; the progress on your phone stays. To delete your account, open You → Delete account in the App. This immediately deletes your GyanMirai account everywhere (on gyanmirai.com and in every GyanMirai app), together with everything synced to it, including your gyanmirai.com profile, website progress and test results. The progress on your phone is kept until you uninstall the App.

While GyanMirai Pro bought on gyanmirai.com (a subscription or an exam-season pass, not the App's "Remove ads") is still running on your account, the App can't delete the account: cancel the subscription on gyanmirai.com and try again once your Pro has ended, or email hello@gyanmirai.com and we will delete the account for you. You can ask us to delete your account by email at any time; see how to delete your account.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought "Remove ads", the App shows banner ads on the Home and Browse screens and a full-screen ad when you leave a finished lesson (never after your first lesson). Ads are never shown inside a lesson, a review or writing practice. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device model and operating system
  • ad impressions, clicks and diagnostic data

Google uses this data to show, measure and (with your consent) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from You → Ad privacy choices in the App.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Buying "Remove ads" stops all ad requests from the App.

5. Purchases

"Remove ads" is an optional one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play under its own privacy policy. We never receive your card or bank details.

To confirm and restore your purchase, the App uses RevenueCat, which receives the store purchase receipt, an app user ID and basic device information (model, OS version, app version, country). If you are not signed in, the app user ID is an anonymous ID generated on your device; if you are signed in, it is your account's user ID, so your purchase follows your account. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Links to our website

Some cards in the App (for example the GyanMirai JLPT mock-test banner and this privacy policy) open gyanmirai.com in your browser. These links carry campaign tags (such as utm_source=kana_app) so we can see that the visit came from the App; they contain no identifier for you. What happens on the website is covered by our website Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The App is intended for teenagers and adults (13+) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security and retention

Data sent by the App travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and synced data can only be read by the account it belongs to. Study data on your device stays until you uninstall the App or clear its data.

If you sign in, your account and synced progress are kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, it is removed from our servers straight away, and from any backups within 30 days. Data held by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies linked above.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. If you have not signed in, we do not hold your study data on any server, so you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. If you have an account, you can delete it and its synced data: use You → Delete account in the App or follow the steps on our account deletion page. For advertising data, use the ad choices above or Google's own controls. For access, correction or anything else, email us (from your account's email address if it is about your account) and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes

If we change how the App handles your data, we will update this policy before the new version is released and change the effective date above.
